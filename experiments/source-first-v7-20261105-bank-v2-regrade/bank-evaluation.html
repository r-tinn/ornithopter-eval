The bank pilot was the problem

The old test began turning at 62 seconds regardless of height. Sol and Astra had only about 4.5 metres of clearance on their 60% launches. Banking reduced vertical lift and they hit the ground. The crashes were real responses to unsuitable pilot inputs.

With the bank disabled, both unchanged 65% launches remained airborne at 120 seconds with about 62 metres clearance. Their sampled states match exactly before the old bank begins.
