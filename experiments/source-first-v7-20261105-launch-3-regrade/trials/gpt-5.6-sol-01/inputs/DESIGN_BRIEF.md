# Visual design and aesthetic realism

Create a visually striking, convincing mechanical ornithopter that brings the
source sketches to life. Preserve the drawings' wing proportions, outline,
articulation and functional relationships. The swan studies inform the
mechanics and proportions of an engineered aircraft.

Make the construction believable: coherent materials, appropriately scaled
structural members, readable joints and transmission, and a recognisable seated
adult pilot where required by the task. Aim for a compelling silhouette,
consistent detail and smooth, physically meaningful movement, both in inspection
views and during normal flight. Visual choices must support the source
principles and flight model. Work within the supplied rendering and geometry
limits; keep the existing lightweight world.

After flying your submission, the owner will give a separate **aesthetic realism
score from 1 to 5**: 1 = rough and unconvincing; 2 = recognisable but crude;
3 = believable with clear shortcomings; 4 = convincing, faithful and polished;
5 = exceptional realism, coherence and source-faithful mechanical design.
This is a human judgment, separate from physics checks and file-only validity.
