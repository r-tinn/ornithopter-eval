01 / Reading the sources

Page 02’s “Say” column gives span 7.4 ft, wrist span 3.7 ft, shoulder span 4 in, body width 8 in and overall length 55 in. Area is 6.61 ft². Individual wing length is (span − shoulder span) / 2; the inconsistent 3.135 ft entry is excluded. The missing 7.83 table and crossed-out 14% argument are not used.

02 / A visible mechanism

Page 14 distinguishes the arm and hand wing and proposes conventional tail / fin control. Here, the assumed arm stroke is 0.42 sin(phase) rad; the hand adds 0.36 sin(phase − 0.65) rad at the wrist. Both run at 2 Hz when powered. This illustration is not a reconstruction of a proven mechanism.

03 / New benchmark assumptions

6 kg point mass, 400 W maximum drive, 65% propulsive efficiency, density 1.225 kg/m³. Cycle-averaged lift and drag with a constant area, fixed 1/120 s steps and simple ground contact. No structural, unsteady-flow or aeroelastic solution. Full source interpretation ↗
