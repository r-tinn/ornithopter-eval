# Biomimetic source packet — mechanical-v4 candidate

These two unchanged family photographs are observations and calculations used
to think about ornithopter engineering, not a complete mechanical blueprint.

- **02 / Unknown-2.jpg:** derive AR from the rounded “Say” span and the original
  wing-area paragraph. Derive wrist-span / full-span from that same column.
  Transfer those dimensionless proportions to the stated mechanical span.
  The inner wing is broadly parallel-chord and the outer wing tapers.
- **14 / Unknown-14.jpg:** inner/arm versus outer/hand articulation, and the
  explicit discussion of conventional tail/fin/rudder control for an ornithopter.
  Use this as engineering context, not a solved force or mechanism specification.

Do not use the erroneous “3.135 ft” wing length, unavailable 7.83 comparison or
crossed-out area argument on page 02. The animal's body width, neck and overall
length are not prescribed mechanical airframe dimensions. The exact biological
stroke angles, spring stiffness, curved-plate polar and vortex-landing hypothesis
are not quantitative requirements: their reference frames or data are incomplete.

The 3 m span, 6 kg mass, 400 W drive, 2 Hz cycle, gear geometry and educational
polar are new proposed benchmark assumptions, explicitly listed in engineering.json.
They are not attributed to Stanley and do not demonstrate real-world flight.
